Privacy Policy for Alchemetric: The Great Work

Effective date: November 18, 2026
Last updated: November 18, 2026

This policy describes how the game Alchemetric: The Great Work ("Alchemetric", "the game", "we", "us") handles information. It applies to the game as distributed through Google Play and the Apple App Store, and to any web version of the same game.

The short version: the game itself does not collect, sell, or share your personal data, and it has no accounts or sign-up. The Android version shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google collects certain data to serve those ads, as described in Section 2.

1. Information the game itself collects

None. The game:

  • has no user accounts and no sign-up or login;
  • does not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal information;
  • sends no data to us — we have no servers and receive nothing from the game. The game internally notes gameplay events (such as a discovery being made) to run its own features on your device; those events stay on your device and are not sent to us.

2. Advertising — Google AdMob (Android version)

The Android version of Alchemetric is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google's advertising software in the game may collect and share the following, under Google's own privacy policy:

  • Device or other IDs — your device's advertising ID and similar identifiers;
  • Approximate location — inferred from your IP address, at city/region level, never precise GPS;
  • App interactions — information about how the app is used in connection with ads (such as ad impressions and taps);
  • Diagnostics — app performance and crash-related data connected with ad serving.

Google uses this data for advertising and marketing, analytics, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Data AdMob transmits is encrypted in transit. We do not receive, store, or control this data, and we cannot delete it — it is held by Google.

Your choices:

  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of ad personalization, in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads on Android).
  • Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), the game shows a consent message before personalized ads are served, and you can change your choice there.

How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Information stored on your device

To work as a game, Alchemetric stores the following only on your device, in the app's local storage:

  • your game progress (elements discovered and related game state);
  • a backup copy of that progress, used automatically if the main copy is damaged;
  • a list of the content packs you own, so purchased content stays unlocked.

This information never leaves your device except through the save-code feature described in Section 5, which only you can trigger.

4. In-app purchases

Alchemetric offers optional content packs as in-app purchases (for example, the "Winter Wonders" pack, product ID pack_winter).

  • All purchases are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store, under that store's terms and privacy policy.
  • We never see, receive, or store your payment details (such as card numbers). The store tells the game only whether a purchase succeeded and which packs your store account owns, so the game can unlock them and restore them if you reinstall.
  • Refunds, payment questions, and purchase records are handled by Google or Apple through your store account, not by us.

5. Save codes

The game can generate a save code that represents your progress, so you can move your game to another device. Save codes are created on your device, only when you choose to create one, and are copied by you (for example, by pasting the code somewhere of your choosing). We do not receive save codes, and a save code contains game progress only — no personal information.

6. How to delete your data

  • Your game data lives only on your device: use "Start over" in the game to erase progress, or clear the app's data / uninstall the app to erase everything the game stores locally, including the owned-pack list. Uninstalling does not cancel or refund purchases; your store account keeps your purchase record, and packs can be restored through the game's restore function.
  • AdMob data is held by Google, not by us. Use the device controls in Section 2 (reset/delete advertising ID) and Google's own controls (such as myaccount.google.com) to manage or delete it.

7. Children

Alchemetric is a general-audience puzzle game rated for ages 18 and over in its store listing. The game itself collects no personal data from anyone, including children. Advertising data handling by Google AdMob is described in Section 2. In-app purchases are made through a store account and are subject to that store's parental controls and consent rules. If you believe a child has somehow provided us with personal information (for example, by emailing us), contact us using the address in Section 10 and we will delete it.

8. Sharing and selling data

We do not sell personal information, and we have none of yours to share. The external parties involved in operating the game are Google and Apple as app stores and payment processors, and Google AdMob as the advertising provider (Section 2), each acting under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy

9. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how information is handled, we will update this policy first, publish the new version at this same address, and change the "Last updated" date at the top. The version in force is always the one posted here.
