Summary

Alchemetric is a fully offline game. The game file makes no network requests: it never contacts a server, never sends analytics, and never tracks you. Your progress is saved only on your own device. Purchases of content packs are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store; we never see your payment information.
