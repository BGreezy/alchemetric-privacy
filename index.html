Privacy Policy for Alchemetric: The Great Work

Effective date: November 1, 2026
Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy describes how the game Alchemetric: The Great Work ("Alchemetric", "the game", "we", "us") handles information. It applies to the game as distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play, and to any web version of the same game.

The short version: Alchemetric does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data. The game runs entirely on your device and makes no network requests.

1. Information we collect

None. The game:

  • has no user accounts and no sign-up or login;
  • does not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal information;
  • makes no network requests and sends no data to us or to any third party;
  • contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking software that transmits data off your device. The game internally notes gameplay events (such as a discovery being made) to run its own features on your device; those events stay on your device and are not sent anywhere.

2. Information stored on your device

To work as a game, Alchemetric stores the following only on your device, in the app's / browser's local storage:

  • your game progress (elements discovered and related game state);
  • a backup copy of that progress, used automatically if the main copy is damaged;
  • a list of the content packs you own, so purchased content stays unlocked.

This information never leaves your device except through the save-code feature described in Section 4, which only you can trigger. We have no servers and cannot access this information.

3. In-app purchases

Alchemetric offers optional content packs as in-app purchases (for example, the "Winter Wonders" pack, product ID pack_winter).

  • All purchases are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play, under that store's terms and privacy policy.
  • We never see, receive, or store your payment details (such as card numbers). The store tells the game only whether a purchase succeeded and which packs your store account owns, so the game can unlock them and restore them if you reinstall.
  • Refunds, payment questions, and purchase records are handled by Apple or Google through your store account, not by us.

4. Save codes

The game can generate a save code that represents your progress, so you can move your game to another device. Save codes are created on your device, only when you choose to create one, and are copied by you (for example, by pasting the code somewhere of your choosing). We do not receive save codes, and a save code contains game progress only — no personal information.

5. How to delete your data

Because all game data lives on your device, you control it completely:

  • Erase your progress in the game: use "Start over" in the game. This clears your progress. It does not remove your record of owned content packs, so purchased packs stay available to you.
  • Erase everything the game stores locally, including the owned-pack list: clear the app's data in your device settings, uninstall the app, or — in a web browser — clear site data / local storage for the game. Note that uninstalling or clearing data does not cancel or refund purchases; your store account keeps your purchase record, and packs can be restored through the game's restore function, which asks the store what you own.
  • No request to us is needed to delete data, because we hold none of your data.

6. Children

Alchemetric is a general-audience puzzle game. Because the game collects no personal data from anyone, it collects no personal data from children either. In-app purchases are made through a store account and are subject to that store's parental controls and consent rules. If you believe a child has somehow provided us with personal information (for example, by emailing us), contact us using the address in Section 9 and we will delete it.

7. Sharing and selling data

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties — we have none to share. The only external parties involved in operating the game are Apple and Google as app stores and payment processors, acting under their own privacy policies:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

8. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how information is handled (for example, if an analytics service is connected), we will update this policy first, publish the new version at this same address, and change the "Last updated" date at the top. The version in force is always the one posted here.
